Acknowledgement of Country

Before you begin

I acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands upon which this digital portfolio exists and was presented. I recognise the way in which they continue to connect to country, culture, family and education, and I pay my respects to both past and present Elders.

I honour Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the very first teachers and knowledge holders of these lands. Their cultures, languages, connections and actions remain strong and enduring. Sovereignty was never ceded, and this shapes the importance of respectful and inclusive practice with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children, families and communities throughout this resource.

Working with Children, Families and Communities in Contemporary Society
